# Mimi Cherry Private 

Create the homepage only for a luxury handbag brand named 'Mimi Cherry Private'. Do not use placeholder brand names. The brand name must appear everywhere as Mimi Cherry Private.

Creative direction:
Mimi Cherry Private is not a quiet luxury clone. The brand combines refined luxury with sculptural creativity. The products feature distinctive curves, organic silhouettes, feminine lines, expressive colors, and signature shapes. The visual identity is inspired by the flowing curves of the MC monogram.

Design style:
- Premium luxury fashion house
- Contemporary African luxury
- Sculptural elegance
- Feminine sophistication
- Editorial fashion photography
- Minimal but not cold
- Organic curves and soft geometry
- Mobile-first

Color palette:
- Ivory backgrounds
- Warm off-white
- Deep black typography
- Soft gold accents
- Accent colors inspired by the products: burgundy, blush pink, celadon blue, sage green
Use accent colors sparingly.

Typography:
Elegant luxury serif for headlines and clean sans-serif for body text.

Homepage structure:
1. Hero section using real brand photography. Focus on the shape and silhouette of the handbags rather than generic luxury imagery.
Headline example: 'L’élégance dessinée autrement.'
Subheadline about contemporary handbags designed for women who appreciate beautiful and distinctive pieces.
Primary CTA: Découvrir la collection.
Secondary CTA: Notre histoire.

2. Signature Collection section immediately after hero. Showcase the most iconic handbag designs. Large imagery with emphasis on curves and form.

3. Brand Story section. Do not mention Paris. Tell a story rooted in Dakar, contemporary femininity, design, confidence, and elegance.

4. Brand Values section with custom line-style icons inspired by the MC monogram curves. Values:
- Qualité exceptionnelle
- Design signature
- Matériaux premium
- Production limitée

5. Featured products section.

6. Editorial campaign banner.

7. Instagram/community section.

8. Newsletter.

9. Premium footer.

Requirements:
- Entire site language must be French.
- Currency must be FCFA, not Euro.
- Avoid generic AI-generated luxury copy.
- Avoid generic stock imagery.
- Emphasize the unique sculptural forms of the handbags.
- The website should feel like a real luxury fashion brand with a strong creative identity, not a Polène copy.
- Use generous white space and premium visual hierarchy.
